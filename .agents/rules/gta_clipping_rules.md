# GTA 6 Viral Clipping & Copyright Compliance Rules

## 1. Copyright Protection & Fair Use Guidelines
- Never re-upload raw, unedited 16:9 gameplay clips or official trailers without transformative modification.
- Always enforce the **Anti-Copyright Shield** unless specifically overridden:
  - Micro-tempo speedup: `1.025x` to `1.035x` to disrupt acoustic matching.
  - Subtle audio equalization (bass punch + high shelf limiter).
  - Visual color grade adjustment (`contrast=1.07`, `saturation=1.10`) and edge vignette.
  - 9:16 vertical re-framing (Split-screen or blurred ambient background) which renders the video fundamentally transformative under fair use.
  - Burn dynamic subtitles and branded visual overlays.

## 2. Virality Heuristics (3-Second Rule)
- High-performing shorts on Instagram Reels and TikTok require high energy in the first 3 seconds.
- Segments must start immediately before a punchy sentence, loud audio spike, or fast cut.
- Discard segments with more than 4 seconds of flat dead silence or static camera angles.
- Optimal short duration: 30 to 45 seconds. Longer than 60 seconds drops viewer retention sharply.

## 3. Subtitle Formatting (Hormozi / MrBeast Standard)
- Subtitles must be UPPERCASE, high-contrast, bold font (`Arial Black` or `Montserrat ExtraBold`).
- Active spoken word must be highlighted in electric yellow (`#FFE500`) or neon green (`#00FF66`).
- Max 3 to 4 words per subtitle card to prevent text crowding on mobile screens.
- Keep subtitles vertically centered around `Y = 1260px` (above UI buttons like like/comment/share, but below video focal points).

## 4. Multi-Platform SEO Rules
- **Instagram Reels**: First line must be a curiosity hook. Include 20-25 targeted hashtags combining macro gaming tags (#GTA6, #RockstarGames) and micro community tags (#GTA6Clips, #GamingReels).
- **YouTube Shorts**: Title must be under 55 characters, include 1-2 emojis, and end with `#shorts`. Description must contain naturally integrated search keywords.
- **TikTok**: Keep caption short (< 100 chars) with a punchline and viral sound recommendation.
- **Rumble**: Descriptive, evergreen, search-engine friendly title.
